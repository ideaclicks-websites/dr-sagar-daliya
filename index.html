Frequently asked questions

When should I see an orthopedic doctor for knee pain?

See a doctor if knee pain lasts more than a week, swells, makes stairs difficult, or wakes you at night. Sudden pain after a fall or twist needs prompt review.

What is short stay knee replacement surgery?

It is knee replacement planned around early walking and a shorter hospital stay, supported by modern anaesthesia, pain control and structured physiotherapy from day one.

Do I always need surgery for arthritis or back pain?

No. Many patients improve with medication, exercise, physiotherapy and lifestyle changes. Surgery is advised only when other treatment has not helped enough.

How long does recovery take after joint replacement?

Most people walk with support within a day or two. Full recovery varies, often several weeks to a few months, and depends on rehabilitation and the joint treated.

What is subvastus knee replacement?

It is a muscle-sparing approach where the knee is replaced without cutting the thigh muscle. It is associated with less pain after surgery, faster recovery and discharge within 48 hours.
